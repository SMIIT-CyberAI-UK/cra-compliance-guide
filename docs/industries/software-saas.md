# CRA — Software & SaaS

The decisive question: is your software a **product** (in scope) or a **service** (usually out)?

## In scope
- **Software placed on the market as a product** (downloadable/installable, firmware) → full CRA
  obligations by **11 Dec 2027**, reporting from **11 Sep 2026**.

## Usually out (check elsewhere)
- **Pure SaaS** → generally outside product scope; check **NIS2** and (finance) **DORA**.
- **Non-commercial open-source** → exempt; commercial support → **open-source steward** regime.

## The load-bearing requirements
- **SBOM** and a **secure-update mechanism** baked into **CI/CD**.
- Vulnerability handling + CVD policy across the support period.

## Overlaps
- AI features → also the **AI Act** (companion guide).
- **GDPR** for personal data.

## Sequence
Now: scope each shipped product; reporting readiness. 2026–27: SBOM + secure updates in CI/CD;
conformity assessment by class. 11 Dec 2027: CE marking.

*Not legal advice — product vs service line is decisive; document it.*
