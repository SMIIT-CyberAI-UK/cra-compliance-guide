# CRA — Automotive Ecosystem (non-vehicle)

**Vehicles themselves are carved out** of the CRA (type-approval governs) — but the connected
*ecosystem* around them is in scope.

## In scope (the ecosystem)
- **EV chargers, telematics dongles, connected aftermarket accessories, companion apps and
  software** placed on the market → **products with digital elements** → CRA obligations
  (reporting 11 Sep 2026; full application 11 Dec 2027).

## Out of scope (the vehicle)
- The vehicle and its type-approved systems → **UNECE R155/R156** and type-approval, not the CRA.

## Practical split
Maintain a register that clearly separates **type-approved vehicle systems** from **CRA ecosystem
products** — they follow different regimes and different conformity paths.

## Overlaps
- AI safety components in the vehicle → **AI Act Annex I** (companion guide); ISO/SAE 21434.

*Not legal advice — the vehicle/ecosystem boundary drives scope.*
