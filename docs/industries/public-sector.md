# CRA — Public Sector (mainly procurement)

Public bodies are rarely manufacturers — their main CRA lever is **procurement**: you inherit
cyber risk from the connected products you buy.

## Procurement duties
- Require **CRA compliance** (CE marking, SBOM, secure updates, defined support period, CVD) in
  tenders and contracts.
- Prefer products from manufacturers that can evidence Annex I conformity and Art 14 reporting
  readiness.

## Where you may be directly in scope
- Government-operated connected products or software **placed on the market** can bring direct
  manufacturer obligations.

## Overlaps
- **NIS2** covers many public-administration entities (entity-level obligations).
- Procured AI → **AI Act** deployer duties (companion guide); **GDPR** for personal data.

## Sequence
Now: embed CRA clauses in new procurements. 2026–27: build a compliance-evidence requirement into
vendor onboarding.
