# CRA — Connected Health (non-device)

**Medical devices are carved out** of the CRA (MDR/IVDR governs cybersecurity) — but a large
"connected health" category around them is in scope.

## In scope
- **Wellness wearables, hospital IoT, practice-management hardware/software, connected
  non-device products** placed on the market → CRA obligations (reporting 11 Sep 2026; full
  application 11 Dec 2027).

## Out of scope
- **Medical devices** (MDR/IVDR) — including their cybersecurity requirements.

## The tricky boundary
The device/non-device line decides which regime applies. A wellness wearable is usually CRA; the
same sensor cleared as a medical device is MDR/IVDR. Classify per product and document it.

## Overlaps
- Clinical AI → **AI Act** (companion guide). **GDPR** special-category health data.
- **NIS2** for health-sector essential entities.

*Not legal advice — verify the device classification first.*
