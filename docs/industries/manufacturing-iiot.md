# CRA — Manufacturing & Industrial IoT

The heaviest CRA exposure: connected equipment, controllers, gateways, and industrial software.

## In scope
- **Connected industrial equipment, PLCs, gateways, and software** placed on the EU market are
  **products with digital elements** → full obligations by **11 Dec 2027**, **reporting from
  11 Sep 2026**.
- Watch **Annex III class** items (network management, industrial control components) → stricter
  conformity routes.

## The hard parts (OT reality)
- Long-lived assets + patch constraints make the **secure-update mechanism** and **support
  period** the toughest requirements — start now.
- **SBOM** across a deep OT component tree; flow requirements down the supply chain.

## Overlaps
- **NIS2** for operators of essential services.
- AI safety components in machinery → also **AI Act Annex I** (companion guide); IEC 62443 for OT.

## Sequence
Now: scope register + reporting readiness. 2026–27: SBOM, secure-update, vuln handling, supplier
flow-down. 11 Dec 2027: full conformity + CE marking.
