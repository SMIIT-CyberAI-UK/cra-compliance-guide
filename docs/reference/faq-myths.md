# FAQ & Myth-Busting — Cyber Resilience Act

**"The CRA is a 2027 problem."**
The essential requirements are — but **Article 14 reporting starts 11 Sep 2026**, an
operational 24-hour-clock obligation you must execute this year.

**"SaaS is covered by the CRA."**
Generally not as a *product*. Check **NIS2** and (for finance) **DORA** instead.

**"Open-source is exempt."**
Non-commercial OSS largely is. If you **monetise or commercially support** it, the lighter
**open-source steward** regime applies.

**"We can start the notified-body assessment now."**
Often not yet — as of mid-2026 **no CRA notified bodies** were listed and **no harmonised
standard** was published. Do the internal work now; the third-party route may be blocked for
higher-class products.

**"CRA only covers consumer gadgets."**
No — it covers hardware *and* software, from consumer IoT to industrial systems, wherever
there's a direct or indirect network connection.

**"Our vehicles/medical devices need CRA compliance."**
They're **carved out** (type-approval, MDR/IVDR) — but connected **accessories, apps, and
non-device products** around them can be in scope.

**"One incident, one report."**
Rarely. The same event can trigger CRA, NIS2, GDPR, AI Act and DORA — see
[reporting clocks](incident-reporting-clocks.md).
