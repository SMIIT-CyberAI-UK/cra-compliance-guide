# Am I in Scope of the CRA? — 60-Second Router

- Do you **place a product with digital elements** (hardware or software that connects to a
  device/network) on the EU market? → **Manufacturer** duties (heaviest).
- Do you **import** such products from outside the EU? → **Importer** — verify compliance first.
- Do you **distribute** them? → **Distributor** — due-care duties.
- Are you a **non-EU manufacturer**? → appoint an **authorised representative**.
- Do you **commercially support open-source** software? → lighter **open-source steward** regime.

### Not in scope here — check elsewhere
- **Medical device / vehicle / aircraft?** → sector law (MDR/IVDR, type-approval) governs.
- **Pure SaaS?** → generally outside CRA product scope; check **NIS2** and (finance) **DORA**.
- **Product contains AI?** → also the **EU AI Act** (companion guide).
- **Essential/important entity?** → also **NIS2** (entity-level obligations).

**Output:** confirm your role, build the product-in-scope register, and start
[reporting readiness](../reporting-article-14.md) for the 11 Sep 2026 deadline.
