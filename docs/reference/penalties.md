# Penalties — Cyber Resilience Act

Fines are the *higher* of the fixed amount or the % of **worldwide annual turnover**.

| Breach | Ceiling |
|--------|---------|
| Essential requirements (Annex I) / core manufacturer obligations | **€15 M or 2.5%** |
| Other obligations (authorised rep, technical docs, CE marking) | **€10 M or 2%** |
| Incorrect / incomplete / misleading information to authorities | **€5 M or 1%** |

- Market surveillance authorities can require products to be brought into conformity, withdrawn,
  or recalled.
- Reporting failures (Art 14) sit under the manufacturer-obligations exposure — the 24h/72h
  clocks are enforceable from **11 Sep 2026**.
