# Quick Reference — Cyber Resilience Act (One Page)

## The two dates that matter
- **11 Sep 2026** — Article 14 vulnerability & incident reporting (24h/72h clocks).
- **11 Dec 2027** — full application (essential requirements, conformity, CE marking).
(Plus 11 Jun 2026: conformity-body notification rules.)

## Am I in scope?
A **product with digital elements** (hardware or software) that connects directly or indirectly
to a device/network and is placed on the EU market. Out: medical devices, vehicles, aviation,
non-commercial OSS, pure SaaS (check NIS2/DORA instead).

## Product classes
Default (self-assess) · Important Annex III Class I / Class II (standards or third-party) ·
Critical Annex IV (EU certification).

## The reporting clocks (Art 14)
24 h early warning → 72 h full notification → final report 14 days (vulns) / 1 month (incidents).

## Technical baseline (Annex I)
Secure-by-design/default · no known exploitable vulns at release · access control & auth ·
**secure update mechanism** · **SBOM** · **vulnerability handling** across a defined **support
period** (~5 years typical).

## Conformity
Determine class → meet Annex I → Annex VII documentation → conformity assessment → **CE
marking** → EU declaration of conformity.

## Penalty ceilings (higher of amount or % turnover)
2.5% essential requirements · 2% other obligations · 1% bad info.

## State of play (mid-2026)
No designated notified bodies yet · no harmonised standard yet · ENISA platform in testing.
Do internal work + reporting readiness now; third-party route may be temporarily blocked.
