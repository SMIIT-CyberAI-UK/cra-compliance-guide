# Glossary

**AI Act** — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, the EU's horizontal AI law.
**AI Office** — European Commission body overseeing GPAI and coordinating enforcement.
**AI system** — machine-based system that infers outputs (predictions, content, decisions)
from inputs, with autonomy and adaptiveness (Art 3).
**Annex I** — EU product-safety legislation into which "embedded" high-risk AI plugs
(machinery, medical devices, toys, etc.); obligations apply 2 Aug 2028.
**Annex III** — the eight use-based high-risk categories (employment, credit, biometrics,
essential services, law enforcement, migration, justice, critical infrastructure);
obligations apply 2 Dec 2027.
**Article 5** — prohibited practices.
**Article 50** — transparency obligations (live 2 Aug 2026).
**CE marking** — mark showing a product meets EU requirements; required for high-risk AI and
for CRA products.
**Conformity assessment** — procedure demonstrating requirements are met (self-assessment or
third-party via a notified body).
**CRA** — Cyber Resilience Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.
**Deployer** — a party using an AI system under its authority in a professional context.
**Digital Omnibus on AI** — 2026 amendment package that deferred the high-risk timeline.
**DORA** — Digital Operational Resilience Act (financial sector ICT resilience).
**Deepfake** — AI-generated or manipulated media resembling real people/events; disclosure
required under Art 50(4).
**ENISA** — EU Agency for Cybersecurity; runs the CRA Single Reporting Platform.
**FRIA** — Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (Art 27), required of some deployers.
**GPAI** — General-Purpose AI model (a foundation model).
**Harmonised standard** — technical standard giving a presumption of conformity once cited in
the Official Journal.
**High-risk AI system (HRAIS)** — AI in Annex I or Annex III scope, carrying the heaviest
obligations.
**Intended purpose** — the use a provider declares; central to high-risk classification.
**NANDO** — EU database of notified bodies.
**NIS2** — Directive on cybersecurity of essential/important entities.
**Notified body** — accredited third party performing conformity assessment.
**PDE** — Product with Digital Elements (CRA scope unit).
**Provider** — party developing/placing an AI system or model on the market under its name.
**SBOM** — Software Bill of Materials.
**Single Reporting Platform** — ENISA platform for CRA vulnerability/incident reports.
**Substantial modification (Art 25)** — change significant enough to make a deployer/modifier
into a provider.
**Systemic-risk GPAI** — model above the 10^25 FLOP training-compute threshold (or so
designated), carrying extra evaluation, red-teaming, and incident-reporting duties.
