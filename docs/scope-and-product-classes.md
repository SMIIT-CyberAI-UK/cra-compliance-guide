# CRA — Scope & Product Classes

## Are you in scope?
**In scope:** any **product with digital elements (PDE)** — hardware or software — whose
intended or reasonably foreseeable use includes a **direct or indirect** data connection to a
device or network, made available on the EU market.

**Out of scope / sector-governed:**
- Medical devices (MDR/IVDR); motor vehicles (type-approval); aviation; marine equipment.
- Products covered by equivalent sectoral cybersecurity law.
- **Non-commercial** open-source software (a lighter **open-source steward** regime applies to
  commercial support/monetisation).
- Pure **SaaS** is generally outside the product scope — but check **NIS2** / **DORA**.

## Your role
- **Manufacturer** — heaviest: essential requirements, conformity, reporting.
- **Importer** — verify the manufacturer complied before placing on market.
- **Distributor** — due care; don't make non-compliant products available.
- **Authorised representative** — required for non-EU manufacturers.
- **Open-source steward** — lighter regime.

## Product classes (drive the conformity route)
- **Default (unclassified)** — the majority. **Self-assessment** permitted.
- **Important — Annex III**
  - **Class I** (password managers, VPNs, network management, some IoT) — self-assessment *if*
    harmonised standards are applied; otherwise third-party.
  - **Class II** (operating systems, firewalls, microcontrollers, routers) — harmonised
    standards or **third-party** assessment.
- **Critical — Annex IV** (hardware security modules, smartcard secure elements) — may require
  **EU cybersecurity certification**.

## Output
A **product-in-scope register**: each product, connectivity, your role, exemption claimed, and
provisional class. This register drives everything downstream.

*Not legal advice — class lists are summarised; confirm against Annex III/IV and implementing acts.*
