# Contributing

Corrections and additions welcome — especially product-class examples and sector annexes.

- Cite an authoritative source for every date/article/classification: the Official Journal
  text, an official European Commission / ENISA page, or NANDO.
- Track the **state of play** (notified bodies, harmonised standards, the ENISA reporting
  platform) as it evolves — these unblock the conformity routes.
- Keep the "not legal advice" framing intact; prefer small, sourced PRs.
